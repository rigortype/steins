- **The locale read of a literal printf format carries a fix: `check --fix` spells `%f` as `%F`, `%g` as `%h` and `%G` as `%H`.** The edit rides `effect.envelope-exceeded` and `effect.liskov-widened` when the exceeding label is `global.read.setting.locale` and its origin is a `sprintf`, `printf`, `vsprintf` or `vprintf` call in the enveloped body with a literal format (#1000). It keeps flags, width, precision and `n$`, leaves `%%f` alone, and is exact on the source bytes in a `'`- or `"`-quoted literal, where an escape-spelled letter such as `"%\x66"` is replaced whole; a format that is not a literal, a heredoc and a callee's printf (the finding names an origin elsewhere) get no fix. The output is byte-identical under the `C` locale and always spells the decimal point `.` under any other, which the fix title says; `h` and `H` need PHP 8.0. A function with no envelope has no finding and no fix, and there is no bulk transform. No finding appears or disappears, only the `fix` payload on the findings above, and the post-check confirms the locale read is gone before anything is written.

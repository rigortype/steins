- **A declared interface receiver no longer reports `call.undefined-method` (or `phpdoc.undefined-method`) for a method one of its implementors inherits from a class outside the interface's hierarchy, or answers through an inherited `__call`.** The declared-receiver lane judged each implementor by its own body, so `A $x; $x->m()` reported `A::m()` when the implementor extended a class that declared `m()` or `__call`, and PHP ran the call. Each descendant is now judged by its whole parent chain, as the exact-receiver lane judges a class, and the property lane judges descendants the same way. Findings disappear on interface receivers whose implementor inherits the method or a `__call` from outside the interface's hierarchy, or extends a class Steins cannot enumerate; this includes calls under an `instanceof` guard that rules that implementor out, since the lane does not read guards yet. A `method_exists($x, 'm')` over such a receiver is no longer folded false, so findings inside its body can appear.

- **A positive `instanceof` no longer deletes declared intersection, `callable`, `non-empty-mixed` or nested-union arms it cannot rule out, so a `Foo&Bar $x` inside `if ($x instanceof Foo)` is no longer dumped as `*NEVER*`.** An intersection arm still dies when one of its members is a final class or enum that is not an instance of the guard class. Findings that read the emptied arm list can reappear in such branches.
  - **`PHPStan\dumpType` after a supertype `instanceof` now prints the more specific declared class.** `Dog $d` under `instanceof Animal` dumps `Dog` rather than `Animal`, and `Suit $s` under `instanceof \UnitEnum` dumps `Suit`; a guard narrower than the declaration still dumps the guard class.

- **A positive `instanceof` no longer deletes declared intersection, `callable` or `non-empty-mixed` arms it cannot rule out, so a `Foo&Bar $x` inside `if ($x instanceof Foo)` is no longer dumped as `*NEVER*`.** An intersection arm still dies when one of its members is a final class or enum that is not an instance of the guard class, and a nested union dies only when every member does. Findings that read the arm list can reappear in such branches. `call.undefined-method`, `phpdoc.undefined-method`, `property.undefined` and `property.maybe-undefined` on an intersection arm under such a guard, or after a positive `@phpstan-assert` of a class, are unchanged: that arm proves nothing there, as when it was deleted.
  - **`PHPStan\dumpType` after a supertype `instanceof` now prints the more specific native declared class.** `Dog $d` under `instanceof Animal` dumps `Dog` rather than `Animal`, and `Suit $s` under `instanceof \UnitEnum` dumps `Suit`. The guard class is still printed when the guard is narrower than the declaration, when the more specific class comes only from a `@param`, and in a scope with references, `extract` or by-reference captures.

- **A `@phpstan-assert` that deletes a union arm no longer premises a proof-layer finding (#1107).** After `@phpstan-assert T $v` (or `!OnlyB`, or `!int`, or an `-if-true`/`-if-false` envelope) removes an arm of a declared union, the surviving arms rest on the docblock claim, so a method the survivor lacks is reported as `phpdoc.undefined-method` under `--profile strict` instead of `call.undefined-method` in the default profile; `instanceof` guards and `assert()` statements keep the proof id, and a dump of the narrowed variable now shows `(asserted)`.

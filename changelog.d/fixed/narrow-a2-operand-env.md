- **The right operand of `&&` and `||`, and each arm of a ternary, now see the declared-type narrowing the left operand or the condition proves, as an `if` body already did, so `if ($n !== null && method_exists($n, 'gone')) { return $n->gone(); }` over a class without `gone` no longer reports `call.undefined-method`.** Past `$n !== null` the operand now reads `$n`'s declared arms without `null` (and past `instanceof`, a truthiness test, `!== v`, an enum-case identity or an array-key presence test, the arms those exclude), so a member guard over the remaining classes folds and the guarded body is dead. Findings can disappear inside code such a guard proves unreachable, including an operand or ternary arm after a condition that now folds; a guard on a method the class does have keeps its body live and its findings.

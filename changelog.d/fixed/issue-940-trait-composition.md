- **A class that uses traits is no longer unenumerable: it holds what its traits give it, so arity, the declaration fatals, member absence and visibility, typed property writes and call resolution judge it, and trait, enum and anonymous-class method bodies are walked.** One `use T;` used to silence every member check on the class, so an abstract trait method left unimplemented (`class.abstract-unimplemented`, naming `U::p` as PHP does), a trait method over a `final` parent method (`override.final`, reported at the `use`), `new C()` on a class with its own `__construct(int $a)` and an empty trait (`call.too-few-arguments`), and a typed property a trait declares (`type.property-mismatch`) were all silent. Trait members are now lowered and composed at query time per ADR-0105: the class's own member first, then its traits', then its parent's. A composition PHP rejects or reshapes declines and stays silent as before: two traits giving one concrete method, an `insteadof` or `as` adaptation, a trait that is missing, declared twice or conditional, a `use` cycle, and two traits declaring one property or constant. A trait's method body is walked once with its receiver open, so receiver-independent findings appear there (`call.undefined-function`, argument types against builtins, the `untyped.*` lints) and nothing about `$this`, `self` or visibility does; an enum's and an anonymous class's methods are walked too. A call in an `if` condition (`if (preg_match(…))`) is now decided by its folded value, as the same call in a comparison already was, so a branch it rules out is no longer judged. On the ten public packages the default profile is byte-identical; under `--profile strict` the moves are new possibly-grade `throw.maybe-undeclared` in trait bodies, definite `throw.undeclared` and `phpdoc.param-mismatch` on calls to trait users' own methods, and `untyped.*` on trait and enum signatures (#940, #952).

- **Deeply nested loops no longer take exponential time to check.** The binding-presence pass behind `variable.maybe-undefined` and `phpdoc.maybe-undefined` walked every loop body up to three times, and a body nested in another loop was walked again from each of those, so a `foreach` nest 20 levels deep took 11 s and 22 levels took 43 s (`while` and `for` nests cost less per level and grew the same way). It now remembers what it computed for a loop body entered from the same binding state, and the 22-deep nest checks in under 0.1 s, a 250-deep mixed nest in 0.15 s. Findings do not change: the answer is a function of the body, the entry state and whether the walk is reporting, and a repeat is replayed from the table instead of recomputed. Nesting that deep is rare in real code; this removes a cliff, not a typical cost.

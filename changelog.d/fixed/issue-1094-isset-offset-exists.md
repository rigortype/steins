- **A final `isset` on an `ArrayAccess` offset or a magic property no longer reports the throws of `offsetGet` or `__get`.** PHP asks only `offsetExists` or `__isset` there, so a false `throw.undeclared` (and a false effect) on `isset($o['k'])` and `isset($o->p)` disappears; `empty`, `??`, `??=` and the intermediate fetches of an `isset` chain still charge both methods, as the engine runs them.

- **A member that exists only on an arm a `@phpstan-assert` removed is no longer reported as undefined on the proof layer (#1107).** After `@phpstan-assert T $v` or `!OnlyB $v` deletes an arm of a declared union, a method the surviving arms lack but the removed arm declares is reported as `phpdoc.undefined-method` under `--profile strict` instead of `call.undefined-method` in the default profile, and a property read of that kind is silent instead of `property.undefined`. Every other finding keeps its id: a member undefined on the removed arm too, a receiver a runtime `instanceof` or `assert()` narrows, an assert that deletes nothing, and `!int`-style scalar removals.

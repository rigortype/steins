- **`variable.maybe-undefined` no longer treats a binding that may not run as unconditional (#948).** A name bound only under the right operand of `&&`, `||`, `and`, `or` or `??`, in one arm of a ternary or `match`, or on the right of `??=` is now `Maybe` after the expression, so a later read fires under `--profile strict`; the true branch of `A && B` and the false branch of `A || B` still count `B`'s bindings as bound. New strict findings appear; the default profile is untouched.

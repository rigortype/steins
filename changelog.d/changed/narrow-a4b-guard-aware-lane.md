- **The declared-receiver lane reads positive class guards: inside `if ($x instanceof T)` (or after a positive `@phpstan-assert T`, an assert-if-true or `assert($x instanceof T)`), `$x->m()` is judged only on the classes of `$x`'s declared type that may be a `T`.** Before, the lane ignored the guard on a class or interface receiver, and declined outright on an intersection under a guard, which also silenced every other arm of its union. Findings disappear inside a branch no declared class can enter (`Solo $x; if ($x instanceof Fin)` where no `Solo` is a `Fin`, or a guard class nothing declares), and an arm the guard leaves no class drops out of the message (`A|OnlyB` becomes `A`). Findings appear where the guard rules out every class that declares the method or answers it through `__call`, or the enum that held the arm open; on a guarded intersection with an inhabitant; on a union holding an intersection arm (`(A&B)|Solo`); on a `Project&\Countable` intersection with a project class that is both; and as `property.undefined` or `property.maybe-undefined` on a guarded class receiver. A finding that rests on a class only a `@phpstan-assert` excluded is `phpdoc.undefined-method`, and the property lane and the `method_exists` fold stay silent on it.

- **`date`, `idate`, `mktime`, `strtotime`, `getdate`, `localtime` and `strftime` now carry `global.read.setting.timezone`, and a literal timestamp drops their `nondet.time` (#1000).** Before, the whole time family carried `nondet.time` whatever its arguments, though a call with a timestamp does not read the clock and every call reads the default timezone. Each of these functions now reads the timezone cell on every call; `nondet.time` stays where the timestamp is omitted or `null`, and goes where it is shown not to be `null`: an integer literal, an `int` parameter, arithmetic, a cast, a property declared `int`, or a call whose declared return excludes `null` such as `time()` (which carries `nondet.time` itself), so `date('Y', 0)` and `date('Y', $intParam)` are `global.read.setting.timezone` alone and `gmdate('Y', $intParam)` carries nothing. `gmdate` and `gmstrftime` read UTC, not the zone, so `gmdate('Y')` is `nondet.time` alone. `gmmktime` reads the clock unless all six fields are supplied, `strtotime` only without a base timestamp, and `mktime` keeps the clock at every arity because the DST flag of the current time can decide a repeated hour. A timestamp that may be `null` or that the scan cannot place (`?int`, an untyped or local variable, a named or spread argument list) keeps `nondet.time` and is no `value-dependent-read` gap. `date_default_timezone_get` reads and `date_default_timezone_set` writes the cell, in place of the coarse `global.read` and `global.write`, which still admit the new labels by prefix.
  - A call that reads the zone and the clock is two `effect.envelope-exceeded` findings under a pure envelope, one per label, as `strftime('%A')` already was; a `@phpstan-pure` function over `gmdate($f, 0)` is silent. A project that tolerates `nondet.time` no longer discharges `date($f, 0)`: tolerate `global.read.setting.timezone` or `global.read`. The family is still neither folded nor remembered, and the `DateTime` constructors and `date_create*` keep `nondet.time` until their own slice.
  - On the public corpus `check` is byte-identical under both profiles; `effect-diff` reports the timezone read added to 121 of 28,846 functions (55 of them new information: they call or reach a time function) and the timezone write added to 58, and removes `nondet.time` from one function (`GitHub::getRateLimit`, a cast timestamp), since the other public timestamps are local variables; `transform effects-envelope` writes the same 140 edits, four of them reworded, and 151 refusals list the narrower labels.

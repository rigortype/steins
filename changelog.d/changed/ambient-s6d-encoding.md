- **The `mb_*`, `iconv_*` and HTML functions that take an `$encoding` now carry `global.read.setting.encoding` when it is omitted or `null`, and the accessors, `mb_regex_*` and `mb_ereg*` functions name the encoding cell (#1000).** Before, 42 of them (`mb_strlen`, `mb_substr`, `mb_strtolower`, `htmlspecialchars`, `htmlentities`, `iconv_strlen` and the rest) were uncatalogued and left the call a `no-effect-row` gap. An omitted or `null` encoding is now the proven read; a literal name drops it for the functions that only measure or search (`mb_strlen`, `mb_strpos`, `mb_stripos`, `mb_strwidth`, `mb_substr_count` and six more), the HTML and `iconv_*` functions; and anything else, a variable or a named or spread argument list, is the `value-dependent-read` gap and no label. The 21 `mb_*` functions that rebuild the string (`mb_substr`, `mb_strtoupper`, `mb_convert_encoding` and 18 more) leave a literal name undecided, because an invalid subject reads the substitution character, which `mb_substitute_character()` writes and the cell holds. `htmlspecialchars`, `htmlentities` and `html_entity_decode` return before they look at the charset on an empty subject (or, for the last, one with no `&`), so a subject the call does not show is the gap, and `''` as the charset names the default.
  - `mb_internal_encoding`, `mb_regex_encoding`, `mb_http_output`, `mb_detect_order`, `mb_language` and `mb_substitute_character` read the cell with no argument or `null` and write it with any other (`mb_regex_encoding` was `global.write`). `mb_regex_set_options` reads on every call and writes when given a string, `iconv_get_encoding` reads and `iconv_set_encoding` writes, and `mb_ereg`, `mb_eregi`, `mb_ereg_replace`, `mb_eregi_replace`, `mb_ereg_match` and `mb_split` read the mb-regex encoding and options on every call. `mb_detect_encoding`, `mb_get_info`, `mb_http_input`, `mb_ereg_search*` and `mb_ereg_replace_callback` stay uncatalogued.
  - `default_charset`, `internal_encoding`, `input_encoding`, `output_encoding` and `mbstring.internal_encoding` now map to the cell for `ini_get`, `ini_set`, `ini_alter` and `ini_restore` with a literal name, in place of the coarse `global.read` and `global.write`, which still admit the new labels by prefix. An envelope that declares only `global.write` is exceeded by an `ini_set` of one of them, as for every other mapped name, because the call returns the old value.
  - On the public corpus `check` is byte-identical under both profiles; `effect-diff` reports the encoding read added to 30 of 28,846 functions (9 call an affected function themselves) and one body made exhaustive; `transform effects-envelope` writes the same two edits and refuses 18 more functions that now carry a read and are not exhaustive.

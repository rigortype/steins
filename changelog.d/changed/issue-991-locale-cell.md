- **A locale read is an effect: a printf-family call with a literal `%f`, `%g` or `%G` carries `global.read.setting.locale`, `setlocale` is `global.write.setting.locale`, and `sprintf('%.2f', $x)` is no longer treated as pure, folded, or numeric.** `%f`, `%g` and `%G` render the locale's decimal point (`sprintf('%f', 1.5)` is `1,500000` under `de_DE`), and `sprintf` reached the pure row through the fold allowlist, which predates that bar (#991). Four registry labels are new, `global.read.setting`, `global.read.setting.locale`, `global.write.setting` and `global.write.setting.locale`, so a declared `global.read`, `global.write` or `global` envelope admits them by prefix and `tolerated = ["global.read.setting"]` discharges the read; `localeconv`, `nl_langinfo` and `strcoll` read the cell (they were uncatalogued), and `printf` keeps `io.output.buffer` beside the read; `setlocale` carries the coarse `global.read` because a locale of `''` or `null` takes its name from the environment block, and a call whose only locale is a written non-empty string (`setlocale(LC_ALL, 'C')`) drops it and is the write alone (the environment read narrows to its own label with the environment cell). A `#[\Steins\Pure]` or interop `@phpstan-pure` body that calls `sprintf` with a literal `%f`, `%g` or `%G` is now `effect.envelope-exceeded`, and `transform effects-envelope` writes `@phpstan-impure global.read.setting.locale` for a function whose only effect is that read. The catalog rows (`sprintf`, `vsprintf`, `printf`, `vprintf`) are the upper bound and the call site decides: `F`, `e`, `E`, `h`, `H`, every other conversion and `%%f` never read the locale, which the catalog's `format_reach` parse reports as `read_format` and `format_reads_locale`, and a format that is not a literal is the `value-dependent-read` gap and not a label; see the call-site entry for what that moves. Two value-lane changes ride with it: the fold seam refuses a printf-family call whose literal format keeps the read (`sprintf('%.2f', 1.5)` stays `string` instead of `'1.50'`, while `sprintf('%d-%s', 1, 'a')` and `sprintf('%.2F', 1.5)` still fold), and `sprintf('%f', $int)` and `'%g'` are no longer `numeric-string` (`%e`, `%E`, `%F`, `%h` and `%H` of a proven `int` are). The same value-lane fix also stops `sprintf('%.0b', $int)` and `sprintf('%5.0o', $int)` from being `numeric-string`: `%b` and `%o` truncate their digits to a precision (`''` and five spaces), a hole that predates this change.

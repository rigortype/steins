- **A locale read is an effect: `sprintf`, `vsprintf`, `printf` and `vprintf` now carry `global.read.setting.locale`, `setlocale` is `global.write.setting.locale` with a coarse `global.read`, and `sprintf('%.2f', $x)` is no longer treated as pure, folded, or numeric.** `%f`, `%g` and `%G` render the locale's decimal point (`sprintf('%f', 1.5)` is `1,500000` under `de_DE`), and `sprintf` reached the pure row through the fold allowlist, which predates that bar (#991). Four registry labels are new, `global.read.setting`, `global.read.setting.locale`, `global.write.setting` and `global.write.setting.locale`, so a declared `global.read`, `global.write` or `global` envelope admits them by prefix and `tolerated = ["global.read.setting"]` discharges the read; `localeconv`, `nl_langinfo` and `strcoll` read the cell (they were uncatalogued), and `printf` keeps `io.output.buffer` beside the read; `setlocale` carries the coarse `global.read` because a locale of `''` or `null` takes its name from the environment block (it narrows when the environment cell gets a label). A `%s` of a float also reads the `precision` ini, which has no label yet (the `precision` cell is registered only in the ADR's roster, D4), so a call that drops the locale read later is not thereby free of settings. A `#[\Steins\Pure]` or interop `@phpstan-pure` body that calls `sprintf` is now `effect.envelope-exceeded`, and `transform effects-envelope` writes `@phpstan-impure global.read.setting.locale` where it wrote nothing or a class-wide `@phpstan-all-methods-pure`. The row is argument-blind for now: `sprintf('%d', $x)` carries the read too, until a later slice reads a literal format at the call site and drops it for formats with no `f`, `g` or `G` conversion (`F`, `e`, `E`, `h`, `H` and `%%f` never read the locale, which the catalog's `format_reach` parse now reports as `read_format` and `format_reads_locale`). Two value-lane changes ride with it: the fold seam refuses a printf-family call whose literal format keeps the read (`sprintf('%.2f', 1.5)` stays `string` instead of `'1.50'`, while `sprintf('%d-%s', 1, 'a')` and `sprintf('%.2F', 1.5)` still fold), and `sprintf('%f', $int)` and `'%g'` are no longer `numeric-string` (`%e`, `%E`, `%F`, `%h` and `%H` of a proven `int` are). On the ten public corpus packages `check` is byte-identical under the default and strict profiles, with and without PHP, and `transform throws-envelope`, `loop-to-array-map`, `phpdoc-honesty` and `phpdoc-to-native` are byte-identical; the effect baseline gains `global.read.setting.locale` on 4,184 of 28,846 function summaries (581 call a family member themselves and 3,603 reach one through a callee) and 12 `global.write` summaries narrow to `global.write.setting.locale` and gain `global.read`, and `effects-envelope` writes 95 new `@phpstan-impure global.read.setting.locale` tags, adds the read to 12 existing `@phpstan-impure nondet.time` tags, and stops writing 35 `@phpstan-all-methods-pure` class tags. The same value-lane fix also stops `sprintf('%.0b', $int)` and `sprintf('%5.0o', $int)` from being `numeric-string`: `%b` and `%o` truncate their digits to a precision (`''` and five spaces), a hole that predates this change.

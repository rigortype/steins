- **The `preg_*` functions that compile a pattern now carry `global.read.setting.locale` where the literal pattern asks the locale's character tables, and a pattern the site cannot read is the `value-dependent-read` coverage gap.** `preg_match`, `preg_match_all`, `preg_replace`, `preg_replace_callback`, `preg_replace_callback_array` (its keys), `preg_filter`, `preg_split` and `preg_grep` compile through the tables PHP builds from the process locale once a script has called `setlocale`, so they were not pure; six carried the empty row and `preg_filter` and `preg_grep` had none. A literal pattern reads when it holds `\w`, `\W`, `\s`, `\S`, `\b` or `\B`, a POSIX class other than `[:digit:]` and `[:xdigit:]`, a caseless flag (`i` or `(?i…)`) where the pattern can match a letter, `[[:<:]]` or `[[:>:]]`, or the `x` flag over a byte of `0x80` to `0xFF`; `\d`, `\p{..}`, literal bytes and ranges, `\h`, `\v`, `.`, `\Q..\E` and a `preg_quote`d literal read nothing. The modifier `u` and a leading `(*UCP)` leave the classes (`(*UTF)` alone does not) but not a caseless flag, `[[:ascii:]]`, a name above ASCII under `(*UCP)` alone, or the `x` flag's skipping of what the table calls a space; an `x` comment is not scanned. An array of patterns reads if any of them does, and a pattern that is a variable, a concatenation or a constant is the gap and never a label. A `#[\Steins\Pure]` or `@phpstan-pure` body whose call reads is `effect.envelope-exceeded` at the call, a body whose pattern is unreadable reports only at the strict floor, and the fold no longer folds a `preg_match`, `preg_match_all` or `preg_split` whose literal pattern reads or that the reader cannot parse, since the runner answers under the C tables (an invalid pattern now keeps its declared type). On the ten public packages `check` is byte-identical under both profiles, 241 function summaries gain the read, 4 bodies that were exhaustive become `…?` through a pattern the site cannot read (one held in a local), no body gains exhaustiveness and `transform effects-envelope` adds 3 edits and removes none (ADR-0101 §3.10, #1000).

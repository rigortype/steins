- **The locale readers beyond printf are catalogued with `global.read.setting.locale` where the call reaches the routine that reads it, `number_format` is certified pure at the call site, and `setlocale($c, '0')` is the read alone.** ADR-0021 refused these names because each reads the locale. `basename` now carries the read on every call. The others carry it only where the call as written reads: `ctype_alnum`, `ctype_alpha`, `ctype_cntrl`, `ctype_graph`, `ctype_lower`, `ctype_print`, `ctype_punct` and `ctype_space` and `ctype_upper` over a non-empty string or an `int` from -128 to 255 (a `bool`, `float`, `null`, array, object or other `int` returns `false` before any table), `strnatcmp` and `strnatcasecmp` over two non-empty operands, `escapeshellarg`, `strip_tags` (over a `<`), `parse_url`, `strftime` and `gmstrftime` (for a format with a conversion that names the locale, `%a %A %b %B %c %h %p %r %x %X`, beside `nondet.time`; the numeric conversions read nothing), the sorts under `SORT_LOCALE_STRING` or `SORT_NATURAL` (and, for `ksort` and `krsort`, `SORT_STRING | SORT_FLAG_CASE`, which a data sort leaves undecided because it reads only before PHP 8.2), `substr_compare` when case-insensitive and `pathinfo` unless only the directory name is asked for. A literal argument decides, an omitted one is the parameter's default, a parameter whose declared type rules the read out drops it, and anything else, such as `ctype_alpha($s)` over a string that may be empty, is the `value-dependent-read` coverage gap and never a label. A bare constant flag is read as PHP resolves it, so a namespaced twin of `PATHINFO_DIRNAME` shadows the global one. `ctype_digit` and `ctype_xdigit` stay as they were: C fixes their sets in every locale and no byte moved under any locale tried. `number_format` renders with `%.*F` and reads no setting, so it is certified at the call site (its separators still run `__toString` for an object). `setlocale($c, '0')` queries the cell and no longer carries the write. A `#[\Steins\Pure]` or `@phpstan-pure` body whose call reads is `effect.envelope-exceeded` at the call, a body whose call may or may not read reports only at the strict floor, a discarded literal call such as `basename('/a/b');` is a `statement.no-effect` finding, and `transform effects-envelope` writes `@phpstan-impure global.read.setting.locale` on such a function; on the ten public packages `check` is byte-identical under both profiles, two bodies become exhaustive and the transform adds two tags and removes none (ADR-0101 §3.9, #1000).

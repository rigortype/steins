- **`new DateTime(...)`, `new DateTimeImmutable(...)`, `createFromFormat` and `date_create*` now carry `global.read.setting.timezone` beside `nondet.time`, and the call drops either read its arguments rule out (#1000).** Before, they carried `nondet.time` alone whatever their arguments, though every construction without a `DateTimeZone` reads the default zone. The zone read now goes where a zone object is shown passed (a `DateTimeZone` parameter, a `new DateTimeZone(...)`) or the literal string names its zone and every field (`'@0'`, `'2020-01-01 10:00 UTC'`, `'2016-01-21T21:11:30+00:00'`, `'now UTC'`); `nondet.time` goes where the literal fills every field (`'2020-01-01'`, `'2020-01-01 10:00:00'`, `'@0'`) or a `createFromFormat` format resets them with `!` or `|`. A string the call does not spell, an alias of the class, and a named or spread argument list keep both labels and are no `value-dependent-read` gap.
  - A pure envelope over `new DateTime()` is now two `effect.envelope-exceeded` findings, one per label, and over `new DateTime('2020-01-01')` one, naming the zone instead of the clock. A `@phpstan-impure nondet.time` or `#[Effect('nondet.time')]` envelope over a constructor call is exceeded under `check --profile strict` by the zone read it never covered: name `global.read` or the cell. A project that tolerates `nondet.time` no longer discharges `new DateTime('2020-01-01')`.
  - A zone named by an abbreviation or offset (`'now GMT'`, `'today utc'`, `'now +01:00'`) still reads the default zone, because the fields it leaves to the current time are copied from the default zone's wall clock; only an identifier zone (`UTC` in capitals, `Europe/London`) does not, and so these keep the label.
  - On the public corpus `check` is byte-identical under both profiles; `effect-diff` adds the timezone read to 214 of 28,846 functions and removes `nondet.time` from 143 (literals such as `'@0'` and `'2000-01-01'` in Carbon, Chronos and Monolog tests), with no gap or coverage change; `transform effects-envelope` writes the same edits, three of them reworded to name the zone, and 41 method candidates whose only label was the clock leave the refusal list.

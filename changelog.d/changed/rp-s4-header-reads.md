- **Calls and offset, property and class-constant reads in an `if` or `elseif` condition, a loop header, a `switch` subject or case label and a `match` subject or arm condition are now judged where PHP evaluates them, and a condition PHP never evaluates is no longer reported.** `call.undefined-function`, `call.undefined-method`, `call.too-few-arguments`, `call.on-null`, `class-const.undefined`, `offset.missing`, `property.undefined` and their siblings now fire in `if (missing())`, `elseif ($a['nope'])`, `while ($o->gone())`, `for (…; $a['nope']; …)`, `foreach (A::NOPE as $v)`, `switch ($a['nope'])` and `match (A::NOPE)`, so findings can appear where they were silent; each is judged in the env that reaches the header (an `elseif` after the earlier conditions failed, a `while` condition in the env that holds at its first test). A `for` step and a `do`-`while` condition run only after an iteration completes, so only the environment-independent ids (an undefined function, method or class constant, an arity error) are judged there, and not at all when no iteration can complete; the same holds for the arms of a `match (true)` used as a value. The carriers inside a header are a statement's: the right of `&&`, `||` and `??` and a ternary arm judge only the environment-independent ids, `@` and `isset` stay silent, and a decided `function_exists()` or `defined()` guard silences its arm. Separately, a condition after one decided true (`if (true) {…} elseif (NOPE) {…}`, a later `case` of a `switch (true)` or arm of a `match (true)`) is now dead, which removes false `constant.undefined`, `class.undefined` and `class-const.undefined` findings there. On the ten public packages nothing changes (#935, #936, #949, #1050).

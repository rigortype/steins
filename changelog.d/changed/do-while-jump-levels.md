- **A `do`-`while` body that ends in `return` or `throw` is trusted to end the loop even when it holds multi-level jumps aimed at its own nested loops or `switch`es (#904).** Before, any `break 2`, `continue 2` or similar inside the body made the walker, the terminality row and the binding-presence pass treat the loop as falling through to the code after it. Now only a `break` or `continue` that targets the `do`-`while` itself, an outer loop, a non-literal level or a `goto` does. A finding disappears only where the successor of such a loop is unreachable: a `call.on-null` or `variable.maybe-undefined` on code after it, and a `type.return-missing` on a function that ends in it.

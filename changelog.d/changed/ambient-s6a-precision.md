- **`strval`, `settype`, `implode`, `print_r`, `var_export`, `json_encode`, `serialize`, `var_dump` and `debug_zval_dump` now carry `global.read.setting.precision` where the value they render is a float, and are a `value-dependent-read` gap where Steins cannot tell (#1000).** A float renders through the `precision` ini (`strval`, `settype` to a string, `implode`, `print_r`) or `serialize_precision` (the rest), and Steins gives both the one label. The read is decided by the call: a float literal, a `float` parameter the function never writes, a float constant, or an array literal holding a float (at any depth for `print_r`, `var_export`, `json_encode`, `serialize` and the `var_dump` family, one level for `implode`) proves it; a value shown to hold none (an integer, a string, an array of them, a `list<string>` returned by a builtin) drops it; anything else (an untyped or `array`-typed parameter, an element read, a local, an object, arithmetic) is the gap and no label. `json_encode` with `JSON_NUMERIC_CHECK` or flags Steins cannot evaluate is undecided for a float-free value, and an explicit `$depth` for a float. `number_format`, `round`, `strval($int)` and `json_encode($int)` read nothing, and `(string) $f`, `"$f"`, `.` and `echo` stay unlabelled. Findings can appear and disappear: a pure envelope over `strval($float)` or `json_encode([1.5])` now reports `effect.envelope-exceeded`, `print_r` and `var_export` in return mode keep the read after dropping the output label, `serialize` and `debug_zval_dump` gain a row (their old `no-effect-row` gap is replaced by the reach and read gaps), and on the public corpus a function that renders a value Steins cannot place is non-exhaustive for the new gap (two became non-exhaustive, 775 already were) with no `check` finding, transform plan or proven label changed.
  - **The fold no longer folds a float it would render.** `strval(1.5)`, `json_encode([1.5])` and `implode(',', [1, 1.5])` are left to their row instead of folding under the default `precision`, and `json_encode` with `JSON_NUMERIC_CHECK` does not fold; the ten public packages lose no fold.
  - **A non-finite float is a read too, for the renderers that cut it.** `strval(-INF)` is `-IN` at `precision=3`, so `strval`, `settype`, `implode`, `print_r`, `var_export`, `serialize` and a printf `%s` of one carry the label; `var_dump`, `debug_zval_dump` and `json_encode` write it whole or refuse it and carry none of it.

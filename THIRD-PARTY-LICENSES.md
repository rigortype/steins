# Third-Party Licenses

The licenses of every Rust dependency compiled into the `steins` release binary,
so a binary-only distribution carries the notices those licenses require.

Steins's own terms are **not** here — the core is Apache-2.0 (ADR-0025) and its
license text is [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Dev-only crates (test tooling, never linked
into a shipped binary) are excluded.

_Generated by `cargo xtask licenses`; do not edit by hand. Entries are grouped by
permission notice — each distinct grant appears once, with the list of crates that
use it and **every** one of their copyright notices above it, which is how a
licence requiring "the above copyright notice and this permission notice" reads.
Differences of typography (line wrapping, indentation) are immaterial to that
grouping; a difference of holder is not, and no holder is dropped._
